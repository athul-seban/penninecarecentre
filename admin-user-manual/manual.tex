% ================= INTRODUCTION =================
\mysection{Introduction}

This manual explains how to use the Pennine Care Centre \textbf{admin panel}
--- the private control panel where authorised staff update everything shown
on the public website, without needing a developer.

\subsection*{What you can do from the admin panel}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Edit the text, images, and videos on every page of the website.
  \item Add, update, or remove team member profiles, job listings, and reviews.
  \item Publish blog posts.
  \item Update site-wide settings --- contact details, the announcement
    banner, branding logos, and email configuration.
  \item Review contact form enquiries and job applications, and track their status.
  \item Manage staff logins and control exactly what each person can access.
  \item See a full history of who changed what, and when.
\end{itemize}

\subsection*{Who can access it}
The admin panel is \textbf{not linked anywhere on the public website} --- it
is only reachable by going directly to its address. Access requires a
personal login, and what you see once logged in depends on the
\textbf{role} your account has been assigned (see Section
\ref{sec:users-roles}, ``Users \& Roles'').

\note{Note}{This manual shows every section of the admin panel. If your own
account doesn't show a menu item pictured here (for example, ``Users \&
Roles'' or ``Error Logs''), that's expected --- it simply means your role
doesn't include that permission, not that something is broken.}

\subsection*{Roles at a glance}
Three roles are set up by default. An administrator can change what each
role can see at any time from \textbf{Users \& Roles} --- the table below
reflects the current setup.

\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}l X@{}}
  \toprule
  \rowcolor{brandInk}
  \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Role}} & \textcolor{white}{\textbf{Typical access}} \\
  \midrule
  \textbf{admin} & Everything --- Content, People, Blog, Settings, Contact
    Enquiries, Job Applications, Error Logs, Activity Log, and Users \& Roles.
    This role cannot be renamed or deleted. \\
  \rowcolor{rowGray} \textbf{manager} & Content, People, Blog, Contact
    Enquiries, and Job Applications --- not Settings, Error Logs, Activity
    Log, or Users \& Roles. \\
  \textbf{hr} & Team Members, Job Listings, and Job Applications only. \\
  \bottomrule
\end{tabularx}

% ================= LOGGING IN =================
\mysection{Logging In}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Go to \url{https://admin.penninecarecentre.com/login} in your browser.
  \item Enter the email address and password you were given.
  \item Click \textbf{Sign In}.
\end{enumerate}

\screenshot{01-login.png}{The admin login screen.}

\note{Forgot your password?}{There is no self-service ``forgot password''
link. Ask a colleague whose role includes \textbf{Users \& Roles} to reset it
for you from the \textbf{Users} tab (see Section \ref{sec:users-roles}), or
update it yourself from \textbf{My Account} once you're logged in (Section
\ref{sec:my-account}).}

% ================= DASHBOARD =================
\mysection{The Dashboard}

The Dashboard is the first thing you see after logging in. It gives an
at-a-glance overview of the site:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item \textbf{Summary tiles} --- current counts of Team Members, Job
    Listings, Reviews, Contact Enquiries, and Job Applications, each linking
    straight to the relevant manager.
  \item \textbf{Page Analytics} --- total visits, today/this week/this month,
    the most-visited pages, a 7-day visit chart, device breakdown, and traffic
    sources.
  \item \textbf{Recent Activity} --- the latest contact enquiries and job
    applications.
  \item \textbf{Date Range Report} --- pick any two dates to see visits,
    enquiries, applications, and reviews for that period, with a chart and an
    \textbf{Export CSV} button for your own records.
  \item \textbf{Quick Actions} --- shortcuts to the two most common tasks,
    editing pages and updating site settings.
\end{itemize}

\screenshot{02-dashboard.png}{The Dashboard --- summary tiles, analytics, and quick actions.}

% ================= CONTENT (PAGES) =================
\mysection{Managing Website Content}

\textbf{Content} is where you edit the actual text, images, and videos shown
on the public website --- 13 pages in total (Home, Pennine Suite, Moorland
Suite, Services, Life at Pennine, Team, Admissions, FAQ, Fees \& Funding,
Contact, Careers, Blog, and Privacy Policy).

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Click \textbf{Content} in the left sidebar.
  \item Choose a page from the \textbf{Website Pages} list on the left.
  \item Every editable field for that page appears on the right, clearly
    labelled --- plain text, longer text blocks, images (with an upload
    button), and video links.
  \item Make your changes, then click \textbf{Save}.
\end{enumerate}

\screenshot{03-pages-editor.png}{Editing the Home page --- every field is labelled, and images can be uploaded directly.}

\note{Tip}{Only the fields that exist for that particular page are shown ---
you won't see irrelevant fields cluttering the form. If you upload an image,
it replaces the one on the live site as soon as you click Save.}

% ================= PEOPLE =================
\mysection{Managing People}

\textbf{People} brings together the three things about staff and public
feedback that change most often: \textbf{Team Members}, \textbf{Job
Listings}, and \textbf{Reviews}. Each has its own list on the left, with a
\textbf{+} button to add a new entry.

\screenshot{04-people.png}{The People manager --- Team Members, Job Listings, and Reviews, each with their own list.}

\subsection{Team Members}
Click any name to edit their photo, name, role, and bio, reorder them, or
mark them inactive. Click \textbf{+} next to ``Team Members'' to add someone new.

\screenshot{04-people-team.png}{Editing a team member's profile.}

\subsection{Job Listings}
Click any listing to edit its title, description, and requirements, or toggle
it between \textbf{Open} and \textbf{Closed}. Closed jobs stop accepting new
applications but stay visible in your records.

\screenshot{04-people-careers.png}{Editing a job listing.}

\subsection{Reviews}
Click any review to edit the author name, star rating, and text, or toggle
whether it's visible on the public website. Only reviews marked visible
appear in the homepage testimonials.

\screenshot{04-people-reviews.png}{Editing a testimonial/review.}

% ================= BLOG =================
\mysection{Managing the Blog}

The \textbf{Blog} section publishes news and updates to the public
\href{https://penninecarecentre.com/blog}{Blog page}.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Click \textbf{Blog} in the sidebar.
  \item Click \textbf{+} to write a new post, or select an existing one to edit it.
  \item Fill in the title and content, then \textbf{Save} to publish (or save as a draft, if that option is shown).
  \item To remove a post from the site, delete it from the list.
\end{enumerate}

\screenshot{05-blog.png}{The Blog manager.}

% ================= SETTINGS =================
\mysection{Site Settings}

\textbf{Settings} controls site-wide options that aren't tied to a single
page:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item \textbf{Contact details} --- the address, phone number, and email
    shown in the footer, the Privacy Policy, and the 404 page. Update it once
    here and it updates everywhere automatically.
  \item \textbf{Branding} --- upload a new navbar/footer logo without any
    code changes.
  \item \textbf{Announcement banner} --- the text shown in the bar at the top
    of the website, and a toggle to show or hide it entirely.
  \item \textbf{Email \& SMTP configuration} --- the mail server settings
    used to send notification emails for new contact enquiries and job
    applications.
\end{itemize}

\screenshot{06-settings.png}{Site Settings.}

\note{Note}{Only accounts with the \textbf{Settings} permission can see or
change this page --- by default, that's the \textbf{admin} role only.}

% ================= CONTACT ENQUIRIES =================
\mysection{Contact Enquiries}

Every submission from the website's Contact Us form lands here.

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=6mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Click \textbf{Contact Enquiries} in the sidebar.
  \item Click an enquiry to read the full message.
  \item Update its status --- \textbf{read}, \textbf{replied}, or
    \textbf{archived} --- and add an internal note if useful (e.g.\ who
    followed up and how).
  \item Use the page controls at the bottom of the list to move through older
    enquiries as the list grows.
\end{enumerate}

Applications submitted against a job listing (including any CV/document the

% ================= USERS & ROLES =================
\mysection{Users \& Roles}
\label{sec:users-roles}

This is where staff logins and permissions are managed. It has two tabs:
\textbf{Users} and \textbf{Roles}.

\subsection{Users}
Lists every admin account, the role it's assigned, and when it was added.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Click \textbf{+ Add User} to create a new login --- set their name,
    email, password, and role.
  \item Click \textbf{Edit} on any user to change their name, email, or role,
    or to reset their password.
  \item Click \textbf{Delete} to remove a login that's no longer needed.
\end{itemize}

\screenshot{09-users-roles.png}{The Users tab --- every admin login and its assigned role.}

\note{Built-in safeguard}{You can never delete your own account while logged
in as it --- notice the \textbf{Delete} button is disabled on your own row.
This exists purely to stop anyone accidentally locking themselves out.}

\subsection{Roles}
Lists every role, how many users and permissions it has, and lets you create
new roles for job functions not covered by the three defaults.

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=5mm, itemsep=1mm]
  \item Click \textbf{+ Add Role} to create a new role from scratch.
  \item Click \textbf{Edit} on any role to rename it and tick or untick which
    of the 11 sections of the admin panel it can access: Content, Team,
    Careers, Reviews, Blog, Settings, Contact Enquiries, Job Applications,
    Error Logs, Activity Log, and Users \& Roles.
\end{itemize}

\screenshot{09-roles-permissions.png}{Editing a role's permissions --- one checkbox per admin section.}

\note{Built-in safeguards}{Two rules protect the system from being
accidentally locked down: the \textbf{admin} role can never be renamed or
deleted, and a role can't be deleted while any user is still assigned to
it --- reassign or remove those users first and the \textbf{Delete} button
will become available.}

% ================= ACTIVITY LOG =================
\mysection{Activity Log}

Every time someone creates, edits, or deletes something through the admin
panel, it's recorded here --- \textbf{who} did it, \textbf{what} they
changed, and \textbf{when}. Password fields are automatically hidden from the
record for security.

Use this to answer questions like ``who published that blog post'' or ``when
was this job listing closed'', and as a record of accountability across your
team.

\screenshot{11-activity-log.png}{The Activity Log --- a full history of admin actions.}

% ================= TROUBLESHOOTING =================
\mysection{Troubleshooting \& FAQ}

\begin{description}[leftmargin=0mm, style=nextline, itemsep=3mm]
  \item[``I can't see a menu item I expect.''] Your role doesn't include
    that permission. Ask an admin-role colleague to check your access under
    \textbf{Users \& Roles} (Section \ref{sec:users-roles}).
  \item[``I forgot my password.''] There's no self-service reset link. Ask a
    colleague with \textbf{Users \& Roles} access to reset it for you, or
    change it yourself from \textbf{My Account} once logged in.
  \item[``I made a change but it's not showing on the website.''] Double
    check you clicked \textbf{Save} --- an unsaved edit never reaches the
    live site. If you did save, try a hard refresh of the public page
    (Ctrl+F5 / Cmd+Shift+R) in case your browser is showing a cached version.
  \item[``I can't delete a role or a user.''] This is a deliberate safeguard,
    not a bug --- see the notes in Section \ref{sec:users-roles}.
  \item[``Where do I change the phone number/address shown on the
    website?''] \textbf{Settings} --- it updates the footer, Privacy Policy,
    and 404 page everywhere at once (Section 7).
\end{description}
